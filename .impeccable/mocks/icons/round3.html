Recto icons, round 3: the sun, in Field Day's language

Every icon here is built from the app's own parts: warm paper, the ink date card, the teal accent, brick and ochre priorities, slab numerals and the progress strip. Shown on light and dark desktops, then each at real sizes.

01Date Sunrise

The app's ink date card with the sun coming up behind it. Instead of a date, the card holds the slab R and the day's progress strip, two segments done in teal.

02Day Arc

The progress strip bent into the sun's path across a charcoal sky. Two segments are done in teal and the sun sits where the day has reached, over a page with a brick P1 edge.

03Long Shadow

A P1 card stands on warm paper at dawn, rim-lit by the sun behind it and casting a long morning shadow toward you.

04Sundial R

A sundial on warm paper: the slab R is the gnomon, and its shadow falls across the day's hours (6, 12, 18) while the sun sits in the corner. A teal base line ties it to the UI.

05Horizon Bands

The sun setting into bands of the priority colors (brick, ochre, teal, ink, gray), like the colored card edges in the app. The boldest graphic of the five.
