Horizon Bands, refined

The round-3 original on the left, then four ways to push it. All use only Field Day's palette: warm paper, the ochre-to-brick sun, priority brick, ochre and teal, and ink.

ASliced Sun on a Page

The sun is cut by gaps that widen toward the horizon, so it reads as a sun even at 16 px. It sets behind a Recto page with the app's brick P1 edge, two ruled lines and one task checked in teal.

BNight Edition

The same composition on an ink sky. The sun glows harder and the page catches warm light on its edge. Strongest presence in a crowded Dock, and a natural partner for dark mode.

CCard Slices

The upper half of the sun stays whole; below the horizon the bands become dimensional card edges in brick, ochre, teal and ink, narrowing toward you like a stack of the day's priorities.

DReflection

The sun rises over a thin ink horizon, and its reflection breaks into ripples of the priority colors on the page below. The calmest and most "paper" of the four.
