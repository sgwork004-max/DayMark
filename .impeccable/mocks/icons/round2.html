Recto icons, round 2

Rendered objects instead of flat symbols: material, light, depth and texture, on the macOS icon grid. The strip below shows all five together on a desktop; each one then gets its own row at real sizes.

01Linen Daybook

A cloth-bound planner, front on: teal linen weave, a debossed slab R, an ochre elastic band, cream page edges and a brick ribbon. Feels like a well-made object you own.

02Tear-off Day

A desk calendar block with a binder clip. Today's page shows its P1 header and checklist, its corner curling up to reveal the next day underneath.

03Fanned Priorities

The app's P3, P2 and P1 cards fanned in depth on charcoal, a warm glow behind. The front P1 card has one task checked off.

04Morning Spread

An open book at dawn. Light falls across the right-hand page, the recto, where today's priorities are written; the left page stays in shadow.

05Glass Page

The newest macOS look: a frosted glass page over soft ochre, brick and sky light, with an etched R and the day's progress strip.
