Recto: five app icon directions

Each icon follows the macOS icon grid (824 px shape on a 1024 px canvas). Next to each: real sizes on light and dark desktops, the menu bar size, and a Dock. Small sizes are where an icon succeeds or fails, so judge those too.

01Date Card

The Field Day date card as the whole icon: a teal header with two binding holes, a slab R, and the day's progress strip underneath.

02Recto Page

An open book on teal. The right-hand page, the recto, is lit and holds today's priorities in brick, ochre and teal. The name, drawn literally.

03Priority Card

A single P1 card from the app: brick top edge, one task checked off in teal, two to go. Says "tasks", less "notes".

04First Light

Today's page over a sunrise: the day starting on a page. Warmest and most illustrative of the five.

05Bookmark R

Warm paper, a big slab R, a teal bookmark marking today's page and a brick underline. Simplest and most typographic.
